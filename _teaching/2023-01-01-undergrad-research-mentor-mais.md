**Period:** 2023–2025

Mentored multiple undergraduate researchers (3 students) on projects at the intersection of machine learning and hardware security. Key outcomes:

- One mentorship led to the peer-reviewed publication: **"Systematical Evasion From Learning-Based Microarchitectural Attack Detection Tools"** (IEEE JETCAS, Dec. 2024).
- An ongoing project under my supervision focuses on **DimShield**, a runtime defense framework for adversarial attacks against machine learning models, currently under preparation for journal submission.
- Provided weekly guidance on research methodology, paper writing, and experimental design.
