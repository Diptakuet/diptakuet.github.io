**Program:** Academic Program for EXcellence for Engineers (APEX-E), 2023 Summer Cohort

Mentored Sebastian De La Torre, an undergraduate student, in a summer research project titled:

**"Cache Occupancy-based Side-Channel Attack to Fingerprint Docker Images"**

The mentorship involved weekly technical guidance on microarchitectural side-channel concepts, Docker security, and experimental methodology. The project culminated in a **research poster presentation** at the end of the program.
