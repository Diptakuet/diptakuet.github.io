**Courses:** CPR E 491 (Senior Design Project I) — Fall 2022; CPR E 492 (Senior Design Project II) — Spring 2023

Supervised a senior design team of six students who developed a framework to evaluate the robustness of microarchitectural attack detection tools against adversarial AI-based attacks. The team's work included:

- Implementing an end-to-end prototype combining **Intel RAPL-based energy measurements** with adversarial code generation
- Designing a **graphical user interface** for testing and visualizing detection tool performance under adversarial conditions
- Conducting systematic experiments to quantify the robustness of detection models

The mentorship concluded with a **successful poster presentation** demonstrating the working prototype and research outcomes.
