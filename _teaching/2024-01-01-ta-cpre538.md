**Course:** CPR E 538 — Reverse Engineering and Security Testing
**Semesters:** Spring 2024, Fall 2024

Prepared and conducted lab sessions on software reverse engineering, malware analysis, and microarchitectural security concepts. Responsibilities included:

- Designing and delivering hands-on lab sessions covering binary analysis, disassembly, and malware behavior analysis
- Guiding students on using open-source malware analysis tools (e.g., Ghidra, IDA Free, x64dbg)
- Holding office hours to support student learning on reverse engineering and microarchitectural security topics
- Grading exams, assignments, and lab reports
