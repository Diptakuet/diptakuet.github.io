This paper compares multiple hybrid renewable energy system (HRES) configurations incorporating photovoltaic (PV) panels, wind turbines, and diesel generators. We optimize the PV configuration for each hybrid scheme and analyze the effects on energy cost, reliability, and renewable fraction. The results provide practical guidance for selecting hybrid energy system topologies suited to resource-constrained environments.
