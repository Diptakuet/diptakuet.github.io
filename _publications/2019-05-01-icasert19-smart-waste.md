This paper proposes a smart waste disposal system that employs a convolutional neural network (CNN) to automatically identify and classify different types of waste materials from images captured at the disposal point. The system enables automated sorting and routing of waste to the appropriate recycling or disposal stream, reducing dependence on manual sorting and improving recycling efficiency.
