I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Engineering at Iowa State University (ISU). Currently, I am working as a Graduate Research Assistant in the Microarchitecture and Artificial Intelligence Security Laboratory (MAIS Lab) at ISU supervised by Dr. Berk Gulmezoglu for over 4 years. The lab focuses on discovering new side-channel and microarchitectural attacks on Intel, AMD, and ARM devices as well as analyzing side-channel information to build deep learning-based defense mechanisms against such attacks.
